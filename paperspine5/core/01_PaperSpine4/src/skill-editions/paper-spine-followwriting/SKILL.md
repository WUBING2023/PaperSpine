# PaperSpine Followwriting

Identify the real draft or study materials, author instructions, target and protected content. Open the actual sources. Work in a new revision when replacing a substantial draft. Begin with [direct scientific writing](../../skill/references/assertive-scientific-writing.md) and [evidence-led writing with complementary references](../../skill/references/reference-guided-writing.md).

1. Build the paper's own argument around its scientific question, decisive comparison, observed result and warranted contribution. Keep principal comparisons, explanatory analyses and scope tests intelligible where readers first encounter them. Preserve protected results, equations and figures.
2. Read one strong exemplar or assign complementary jobs across two or three real exemplars. Useful jobs include framing the verified gap, explaining a method, ordering evidence and developing the Discussion. For whole-paper work read the relevant exemplars in full when accessible. For an explicit paragraph-by-paragraph comparison, follow the reference's reasoning in order and match each useful step to the study's available evidence. Check whether the rewritten passage actually makes the claimed transition. Preserve independence of the prose, evidence and conclusions.
3. Write the actual manuscript. State observed results directly, name conditions or limits where they change the inference, and integrate contrary evidence. Make the Introduction, Results and Discussion support the same central contribution. A whole-paper request needs inspection of every section.
4. Keep a compact comparison record for substantial changes. Give the revised location, actual original and revised passages, study evidence, and exemplar version and page/paragraph when a move actually transfers. Distinguish research citations from teaching examples. For requested sentence-by-sentence review, cover every requested sentence, including study-specific sentences without a reference anchor. Use short excerpts or precise paraphrases with locators when sharing the record.
5. For a full rewrite, use a fresh writing context and a separate reviewer when available and authorized. Give the writer the complete sources, original author instructions and counterevidence. Let the writer determine the contribution and narrative. Ask the reviewer to recover the argument from the actual draft before consulting the writing notes. Implement supported findings and inspect the affected manuscript or rendered pages. Return the editable revision and the requested reference comparison.

For direct use with a managed installation, see [updates](../EDITION-MANAGEMENT.md) if available. In a full run, use the orchestrator's existing check.
